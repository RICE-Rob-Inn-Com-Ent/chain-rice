## 🎯 Overview

Rice Monorepo is a 100% open-source project maintained by volunteers. Financial support helps us:

- ⏰ Dedicate more time to development
- 🖥️ Pay for infrastructure costs (CI/CD, hosting, domains)
- 🎓 Create better documentation and tutorials
- 🐛 Fix bugs and security issues faster
- ✨ Build new features
- 🎫 Attend conferences and community events

## 💡 Why Fund Open Source?

### For Individuals

**You benefit from open source every day:**

- Your development tools
- The frameworks you use
- The libraries in your projects
- The operating systems you run

**Supporting open source:**

- ✅ Ensures projects stay maintained
- ✅ Accelerates development
- ✅ Improves software you depend on
- ✅ Builds a healthier ecosystem
- ✅ Gives back to the community

### For Companies

**Open source saves your company millions:**

- No licensing fees
- Faster development cycles
- Community-tested code
- Access to talent pool
- Industry-standard tools

**Sponsoring open source:**

- ✅ Ensures business-critical dependencies remain maintained
- ✅ Influences project direction
- ✅ Builds brand reputation
- ✅ Attracts top talent
- ✅ Tax deductible (in many jurisdictions)
- ✅ Reduces long-term risk

**ROI Example:**

```
Typical company using Rice Monorepo:
- Developers: 10 engineers @ $150k/year = $1.5M/year
- Time saved by not building from scratch: 30%
- Value gained: $450k/year
- Recommended sponsorship: $1-5k/year (0.2-1% of value)
```

## 🛠️ For Maintainers

### Setting Up Funding

#### 1. Create FUNDING.yml

File: `.github/FUNDING.yml`

```yaml
github: [mrDinkelman]
patreon: rice-mono
open_collective: rice-mono
ko_fi: mrdinkelman
liberapay: mrDinkelman
custom: ["https://github.com/mrDinkelman/rice-mono/wiki/Crypto-Donations"]
```

#### 2. Enable GitHub Sponsors

1. Go to [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors)
2. Complete profile
3. Add bank account / Stripe
4. Create sponsorship tiers
5. Publish profile

#### 3. Create Sponsor Tiers

**Consider:**

- Individual vs corporate tiers
- Benefits vs effort required
- Sustainable commitments
- Legal/tax implications

#### 4. Promote Funding Options

**In README.md:**

```markdown
## Support This Project

If Rice Monorepo helps you, consider sponsoring: [Become a sponsor →](https://github.com/sponsors/mrDinkelman)
```

**In Documentation:**

- Sponsors page
- Footer mentions
- Blog posts about funding

**Social Media:**

- Announce major sponsors
- Share funding goals
- Thank sponsors publicly

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is Rice Monorepo free to use?** A: Yes, completely free and open source under [LICENSE]. Sponsorship is optional.

**Q: What if I can't afford to sponsor?** A: Contribute in other ways: code, documentation, bug reports, helping others,
spreading the word.

**Q: Can I sponsor anonymously?** A: Yes, most platforms offer anonymous sponsorship options.

**Q: Are donations tax-deductible?** A: Depends on jurisdiction and platform. Open Collective (via fiscal host) may
provide tax receipts.

**Q: Can I change or cancel my sponsorship?** A: Yes, anytime. No long-term commitments required.

### Corporate Sponsorship

**Q: Can we get an invoice for accounting?** A: Yes, we provide invoices for corporate sponsors.

**Q: Do you offer SLAs?** A: Yes, for enterprise sponsors ($10k+/year). Contact us for details.

**Q: Can we sponsor specific features?** A: Yes, we offer custom feature development for larger sponsors.

**Q: How is our logo displayed?** A: Based on tier: README, website, docs. You approve placement before going live.

**Q: What happens if we stop sponsoring?** A: Your logo remains for the duration paid. After that, it's removed at next
update cycle.
